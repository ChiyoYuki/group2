# Problem Statement

## Context

Every year, students participating in Dream Jourrney conduct field research, collect observations, investigate real-world questions, and create a wide range of project outputs. These experiences contain valuable knowledge, creative ideas, and learning journeys that can inspire future participants. However, past projects are difficult to discover, often scattered across different channels, and typically showcase only final deliverables rather than the questions, methods, and problem-solving processes that led to them. As a result, students, educators, and other stakeholders have limited visibility into the full educational impact of these experiences.

## Problem Statement

**How might we create an engaging digital experience that enables students, educators, and program supporters to discover, explore, and lear from the project journeys of previous Dream Journey participants, while preserving and showcasing the long-term impact of their work?**

## End Product Needed

Design and prototype a solution that helps users:

1. Discover relevant projects from previous cohorts.
2. Understand the questions, research methods, challenges, and learning journeys behind each project.
3. Explore final outputs such as reports, creative works, videos, models, and presentations alongside the learning process.
4. Find inspiration and practical ideas for their own field-study plans
5. Browse content across themes such as cuilture, heritage, ecology, architecture, museums, agriculture, and community research.
6. Surface impactful or highly engaging projects for broader learning, recognition, and promotion.
7. Demonstrate educational and social impact to students, teachers, schools, donors, and program partners.
8. Support future growth through a scalable structure that can accommodate new themes, project types, and engagement features.
